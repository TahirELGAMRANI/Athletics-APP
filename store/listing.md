## Full description [4000]

AUI Athletics is the official app of the Al Akhawayn University Athletics Department in Ifrane. It brings the whole athletics community — students, athletes, coaches and staff — into one simple app.

FOR EVERY STUDENT
• Live facility availability: see instantly whether the Gymnasium, Fitness Center, Soccer Field, Martial Arts Room, Swimming Pool, Padel and Tennis Courts are free, in use or closed.
• Official weekly programs for every facility, plus a day timeline.
• Book a bike, the padel court or a tennis court for the same day in a few taps.
• Follow AUI teams: rosters, captains, upcoming games and results.
• Discover clubs, their members and upcoming events.

FOR ATHLETES
• Your personal portfolio: physical test results with scores and ranking, KPIs set by your coach, and your attendance record.
• Read the notes your coach shares with you.
• Rate your training sessions each month and give feedback.
• Book the ice bath (24 hours ahead) and the sauna.

FOR COACHES
• Take attendance in seconds — present, absent or excused.
• Manage your roster, plan games and enter friendly or official results.
• Track progress with your own KPIs and see physical testing rankings.
• Write notes to each player.

FOR THE ATHLETICS OFFICE
• Complete athlete folders including medical, psychology and contract documents (photo upload, private).
• Staff records, inventory with stock alerts, bookings management, club management, transactions and printable team, club and department reports.
• Create accounts for athletes, coaches and staff.

Facilities are open 8 AM–11 PM on weekdays and 11 AM–9 PM on weekends. Students sign up with their @aui.ma email.

Once a Lion, Always a Lion.
